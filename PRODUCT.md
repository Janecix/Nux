# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Small startups and enterprise teams that need dependable sysadmin support but cannot justify the pricing or overhead of a large consultancy.

## Product Purpose

Nux provides specialized systems administration services that keep infrastructure stable, secure, and understandable for teams without a large internal operations function.

## Positioning

Senior, focused sysadmin support without big-consultancy overhead: practical help that fits the scale, pace, and budget of growing teams.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive when infrastructure work is becoming a distraction, a risk, or a staffing gap. The landing page should help them quickly understand the service and start a conversation.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The specific service catalog, technology stack, pricing, customer stories, and contact details are not yet documented. The surface must not invent metrics, customers, certifications, or guarantees.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is Nux. The visual direction is dark theme with a calm, technical, direct voice.

## Evidence on Hand

No public testimonials, case studies, logos, or performance benchmarks are currently available.

## Product Principles

- Be clear about the work.
- Make expert support feel approachable.
- Prefer useful guidance over consultancy theater.
- Respect the budget and context of the team asking for help.
