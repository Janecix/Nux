# Nux visual direction

Nux uses an operations-room visual language: deep ink surfaces, blueprint-like topology lines, soft system green, and small amber attention signals. The tone is calm and exact rather than flashy or corporate.

## Type

Manrope is used for the human-facing voice, with DM Mono reserved for infrastructure labels, statuses, and small system metadata.

## Color

- Ink: `#0B0E0D`
- Paper: `#F4F5F1`
- Muted: `#9EA8A1`
- Signal mint: `#B8F566`
- Attention amber: `#FFB45C`

## Composition

The landing page favors a strong editorial hero paired with a tangible infrastructure map instead of a generic dashboard mockup. Thin rules and compact monospace labels create rhythm; service rows stay open and readable rather than becoming a grid of cards.

## Interaction

Primary actions use mint fill with a small lift on hover. Navigation remains compact, and the mobile menu collapses into a simple disclosure. Motion is restrained and respects reduced-motion preferences.
